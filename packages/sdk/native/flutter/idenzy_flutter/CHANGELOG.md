## 0.1.0 (unreleased)

- Email and SMS one-time codes, password, magic links, anonymous sign-in and upgrade.
- OAuth through the system browser (`ASWebAuthenticationSession`, Custom Tabs).
- Native Sign in with Apple on iOS and Sign in with Google through Credential Manager on Android,
  exchanged for a session with a server-issued nonce.
- MFA challenges and step-up, TOTP enrolment, recovery codes, account and device-list calls.
- Session status as a stream; the refresh token in the Keychain or the Keystore.
